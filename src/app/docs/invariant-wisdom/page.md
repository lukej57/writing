{% callout title="TL;DR" type="note" %}
Universal technical wisdom does not change, so it will be relegated to AI context and forgotten.
Programmers will keep the concrete: the product, the domain, and one organisation.
{% /callout %}

Related: [The Calculus of Maintainable Software](/docs/calculus-of-maintainable-sw).

Technical wisdom that is universal does not change.
It is the same on the next job as on this one.
Because it is invariant, it does not need to be kept in mind.
It is a perfect fit for AI context.
That is where it will be relegated.
Programmers will forget it.

What they will not forget is the opposite.
The product.
The domain.
The specific knowledge of one organisation.
Those are concrete, and they are not universal.
They are the problem being solved, and they change.

The split will get sharper.
More attention on the details of this problem.
Less on the invariant.
Coupling, reuse, what a dependency is — the conceptual part — leaves the person and lives in the context.
